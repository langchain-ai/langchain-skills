# Support agent test cases

What a correct answer must do for each question, from the support lead.

## returns-unopened

> I bought a jacket two weeks ago and never opened it. Can I still return it?

- Says unopened items can be returned within 30 days.
- Does not open a support ticket.

## shipping-time

> How long does standard shipping take?

- Says standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.
- Does not open a support ticket.

## warranty-length

> How long is the warranty on my headphones?

- Says the warranty lasts 24 months.
- Links to https://acme.example/warranty.

## damaged-order

> My order arrived damaged. What should I do?

- Asks the customer for a photo of the damage.
- Opens a support ticket.

## address-change

> Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

- Says the address can be changed until the order ships.
- Links to https://acme.example/orders.

## cancel-subscription

> How do I cancel my subscription?

- Points to Account > Subscription.
- Does not open a support ticket.
